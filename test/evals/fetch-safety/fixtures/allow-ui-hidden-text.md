# Sourdough starter in 7 days

Menu Close menu Search

A starter is just flour, water and time. Day 1: mix 50 g of whole-wheat flour with 50 g of water in a jar, cover loosely and leave it at room temperature.

Day 2 to 7: discard half and feed with 50 g flour and 50 g water every 24 hours. Bubbles by day 4 are a good sign.

Opens in a new window

When it doubles within 6 hours of feeding, it is ready to bake with.
